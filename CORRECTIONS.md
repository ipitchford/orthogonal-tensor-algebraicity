# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to symmetric-determinantal-hilbert-threshold 0.1.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22660867)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v0.1.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/symmetric-determinantal-hilbert-threshold/>.

1. Corrected display (13). The archived text says c_m = A_m π^{−r/2} with A_m ∈ ℚ_{>0}. A_m need not be rational (for example A_3 = 3√6/32, matching the paper's own value of c_3); what is rational is its square. Display (13) should read: c_m = A_m π^{−r/2}, A_m > 0, A_m² ∈ ℚ_{>0}, with (14) read as the formula for A_m².

   The proof uses only that A_m is a nonzero algebraic number, which remains true. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
